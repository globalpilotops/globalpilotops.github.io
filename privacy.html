Privacy policy

Effective date: November 8, 2026

This website is operated by Ramandeep Singh, trading as Global Pilot Ops, in Ontario, Canada. Privacy questions can be directed to globalpilotops@gmail.com.

Information on this website

The website is designed as a static information site. It includes no contact form, accounts, analytics scripts, advertising trackers, or newsletter signup. Email links open your email application.

If you email us

Your email may include your name, email address, message, and any other information you choose to provide. We use this information to respond to your inquiry, discuss potential services, and manage related communications. Do not send sensitive personal information or credentials.

Email and hosting providers

The contact address uses Gmail. The website is hosted on GitHub Pages. See GitHub’s privacy statement and Google’s privacy policy for information about these providers. Providers may process technical or communications data under their own terms, including outside Canada. Website hosting can involve technical logs such as IP addresses and request information even when the website uses no analytics scripts.

Retention

We keep inquiry emails for up to 12 months, unless the information is needed for an ongoing service or a legal obligation. The operator is responsible for reviewing and deleting inquiries in line with this schedule.

Privacy requests

Email globalpilotops@gmail.com to request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information you have provided, to withdraw consent to further inquiry communications, or to raise a privacy concern. We may need to verify your identity before acting on a request. Some information may need to be retained for ongoing services or legal obligations; we will explain any applicable limits.

Sharing and safeguards

We do not sell inquiry information or use it for advertising. We use email and hosting providers to operate this website and manage communications, and may disclose information when required by law. We limit information handling to what is needed for these purposes. No email or online service can be guaranteed completely secure.

Policy updates

Updates will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.

Client projects

This policy concerns the public website and initial inquiries. Data handling for any client automation project must be addressed in the project agreement before access to client systems or information is granted.
