Our services

Automation with
a clear purpose.

Start with a defined process, connect the steps, and keep people in control. The examples below describe potential project scopes, not prebuilt products or promised outcomes.

01

Lead-response automation

Help new inquiries reach the right place with the information your team needs to respond.

  • Acknowledgment emails based on agreed templates
  • Lead routing and updates to a compatible CRM or spreadsheet
  • Follow-up reminders and handoff notifications

Example: a submitted inquiry creates a lead record and alerts the responsible person. Outreach rules and consent requirements are reviewed as part of the scope.

02

Workflow integration

Reduce repetitive handoffs by connecting tools that support suitable integrations.

  • Information transfer between forms, spreadsheets, and business tools
  • Status updates and task notifications
  • Exception routing when a workflow needs attention

Example: an approved intake record creates an internal task. Feasibility depends on the tools, available access, and their technical limitations.

03

Customer support automation

Help teams organize incoming requests and prepare useful responses from approved information.

  • Request categorization and routing
  • Draft answers for common questions
  • Human escalation for uncertain or sensitive requests

Example: a routine question receives a draft reply for review. AI output can be inaccurate; review and escalation requirements are agreed for each workflow.

A scope that fits your business.

Deliverables, fees, third-party tool costs, access, support arrangements, and timing are agreed before work begins. Results depend on the process and tools involved.
